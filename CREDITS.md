## The PSP port: [z2442/oot-PSP](https://github.com/z2442/oot-PSP)

[z2442](https://github.com/z2442) made the decompilation run natively on the PSP, including
the asset converter that prepares the game from your ROM on its first boot. This repository
is a fork of it.

## Ocarina of Time PSP

[JustKidding23](https://github.com/JustKidding23), with Claude (Anthropic's AI assistant)
